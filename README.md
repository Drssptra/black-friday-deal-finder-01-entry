Black Friday Deal Board 2026 — a static deal finder with 45 real Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals, each checked by hand against its store or a deals source on 27 November 2026. Every deal shows the product, store, store link, sale price, usual price, saving, and an honest verdict (NEW LOW / STRONG DEAL / GOOD PRICE / DECENT / ROUTINE SALE) with the evidence. Searchable and filterable by category, store, discount and verdict, with sorting by biggest discount, biggest saving or lowest price; works on desktop and on a phone. Deepest cut on the board: Fire TV Stick 4K at $20 (was $60, −67%); total savings across the board: $7,432.08.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
